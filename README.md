**Lantern Fox — Steal the Moon-Pearl** is a tiny top-down stealth game drawn entirely with Canvas 2D — no external assets, no libraries. You play a quick orange fox sneaking through a moonlit garden patrolled by four Lantern Keepers. Slip past their vision cones, vanish into the green moon-bushes, prowl quietly with Shift so the alert meters fill more slowly, snatch the glowing Moon-Pearl from its shrine, and escape through the north-east gate before a keeper catches you.

## How to play

- **WASD / Arrow keys** — move
- **Shift** — prowl (slower, but harder for keepers to spot)
- **Hide in the green moon-bushes** — keepers cannot see you there unless they get very close
- **R** — restart · **Enter or click** — start
- Steal the Moon-Pearl from the shrine, then reach the north-east gate to win. If a keeper's alert meter fills, it charges — don't let it touch you.

## Play it

Open `game/index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder locally (for example `npx serve game`) and visit the page. Everything runs offline from static files.
